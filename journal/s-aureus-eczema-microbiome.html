S. aureus & the Eczema Microbiome

08 May 20266 min readSKINPIENS · Navy + Cream

The primary bacteria driving eczema flare-ups is Staphylococcus aureus. Our Epsilon range is engineered to keep it in check.

A bacterial overload

On healthy skin, S. aureus is kept at low numbers by commensal bacteria. On eczema-prone skin, its population explodes, releasing toxins that inflame and degrade the barrier — a feedback loop of flare.

Targeted antimicrobial action

Epsilon Ultra Soothing Mist pairs Lassica K014® Postbiotic with colloidal silver to inhibit S. aureus directly, while menthol + menthyl lactate deliver instant cooling relief — attacking the cause and the symptom simultaneously.
